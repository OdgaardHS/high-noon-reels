How the bonus works
  • Respins. When a duel target lands, its reel and every reel to its left are held, and the reels to its right spin again. If the respin brings a new duel target, the same happens from that reel, until a respin brings none.
  • Three or more duel targets on the reels start the bonus, about once every 30 spins. You can also buy it for 10.3× your bet. Half of all bonuses pay more than 7 times your bet.
  • The bonus plays itself. You don't make any choices.
  • Bounty spins. First you get free bounty spins: 5 for three duel targets, 7 for four and 10 for five. Only bounty symbols count in these spins. Every one that lands is added to your bounty. Every bounty spin brings at least one, and about two on average. Each symbol is worth a fixed part of your bet: boot 0.05, horseshoe 0.1, hat 0.25, sheriff star 1, gold bars 5 and diamonds 25 bets.
  • Loading. You and Jim each load a six-shooter from the bullet reel, taking turns one bullet at a time. Every bullet is a multiplier (×2, ×3, ×5 and the rare ×10) or a combat bullet.
  • Steal takes Jim's best multiplier the moment it's loaded. Only your gun loads them: Jim never gets one.
  • Aim makes your next duel shot an aimed one. The drum turns straight to your best multiplier and fires it, so that one is locked in, and it hits the other cowboy's best unfired multiplier. The order carries on from there.
  • The duel. A coin flip decides who shoots first: heads you, tails Jim. Then you take turns. Each gun fires its bullets in order, turning one chamber at a time; the glowing chamber marked NEXT is the bullet it fires next. A chamber that has been shot out is skipped with a dry click, so no turn is ever wasted. The fired bullet is locked in: it counts for good and can't be destroyed any more. Every shot hits and destroys one of the other cowboy's unfired bullets. You're the sharper shot: yours always take Jim's best unfired multiplier, while his hit a random one of your unfired bullets. The duel goes on until every bullet has been fired or destroyed.
  • Payout. You're paid your bounty times your locked-in multipliers, added together, so ×2 and ×3 make ×5. With none locked in you get the bounty. The payout only ever goes up.
  • Winning the duel. If you've locked in at least as much multiplier as Jim when the shooting stops, you win and his locked-in multipliers are added to yours. You win ties. You'll win about 4 duels in 5. Losing the duel never takes anything away: you keep your bounty times your own locked-in multipliers.
Paytable

Wins are shapes made of one symbol. Every shape on the reels pays, so one spin can pay several times, and a symbol can be part of more than one shape. Wanted posters count as every symbol.
