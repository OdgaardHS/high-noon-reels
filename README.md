# High Noon Reels

A western-themed slot machine. Land a bonus and duel Rattlesnake Jim: take turns building multipliers and shooting down your opponent's. Win the duel to cash out with your multiplier.

Play it here: https://odgaardhs.github.io/high-noon-reels/

- `index.html` is the game (one self-contained page).
- `payout-patterns.html` shows the winning shapes.
- `engine.js` and `sim.js` are the game logic and a payout simulator (`node sim.js`).
